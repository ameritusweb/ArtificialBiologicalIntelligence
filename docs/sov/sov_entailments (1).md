## 1. The Entailment Claim

The standard approach to building intelligent systems treats curiosity, language, and inference as separate engineering problems:

- **Curiosity** is a novelty drive, an intrinsic reward signal, a module bolted onto the reward function
- **Language** is a communication layer, added after concepts exist to transmit finished meanings
- **Efficient inference** is an algorithmic problem, solved by choosing the right architecture

SOV dissolves all three framings. When unknowns are first-class structural objects and the topology that holds them is evolved under survival pressure, curiosity, language, and efficient inference are not problems to be solved. They are *consequences* — entailments of the ledger that the system is already running.

The argument for each is different, and all three are better than they first appear. But they share a common root: the evolved structure ships with open variables, open variables pay rent, and the rent-paying machinery generates everything else without additional mechanism.

This document traces each entailment and then shows how all three couple into a compounding loop — the mechanism by which SOV-capable systems bootstrap intelligence from below.

## 2. Curiosity as the Funded Option Book

### 2.1 The standard view and its problem

Standard accounts of curiosity treat it as a drive toward novelty or information gain — a signal added to the reward function to prevent the system from exploiting known rewards and ignoring unexplored territory. The noisy-TV problem is the canonical failure: a naive curiosity drive fixates on maximally random stimuli because they are maximally novel. Patches exist (prediction error, learning progress, empowerment) but they are patches — additional mechanisms compensating for the drive's mismatch with what curiosity actually does in evolved systems.

### 2.2 Curiosity as theta decay on an inherited option book

In an SOV-capable system with an evolved topology, curiosity is not a drive. It is the funding pressure of an inherited option book.

The heritability axiom of ABI — topology inherits, entries don't — says organisms are born with receptor slot templates but without the receipts that would fill them. In SOV terms: the evolved structure ships with open variables. These are not generic novelty detectors. They are typed slots, pre-positioned in the relational structure, waiting for the specific data that would constrain them.

Open variables pay rent. An unresolved slot loses value unless receipts arrive — the option decays. This is theta decay in the options pricing framework: time passing on an unfilled slot is a cost, not a neutral state. The bookkeeping itself drives the organism toward exactly the data that constrains its open variables.

**The drive is the ledger.** Curiosity is not a module. It is the interest owed on inherited ignorance.

### 2.3 Three corollaries that fall out

**Curiosity arrives pre-aimed.** What you are curious about is determined by which slots your inherited topology opens on first contact with the world. Infant fascination with faces, agency, and contingency is not general arousal or generic novelty-seeking — it is the trunk's standard open variables (`?agent`, `?cause`, `?edible`, `?predator`) firing on first exposure to the data that can fund them. The aim is the inheritance.

**The noisy-TV problem dissolves as a theorem.** Noise cannot sustain an open variable. It offers no stable relational position, no consistent constraint, no receipt that survives generalization. A slot that receives only noise fails funding and is archaized — the bookkeeping prunes it. Curiosity-as-slot-pressure disengages from noise automatically, without a patch, because the ledger enforces it. The D1 learnable-surprise multiplication — imposed as a constitutional patch in the design document — becomes a *derived result* rather than a design choice.

**The "aha" gets a mechanism.** The phenomenology of insight — the sudden reorganization, the cascade of consequences, the feeling that many things have clarified at once — is SOV-P3's restructuring cascade made experiential. Closure of a load-bearing unknown propagates through its dependency graph, improving predictions at every connected node simultaneously. This is precisely the kind of large, sudden, non-local prediction improvement that acquires positive valence under the organism's own value-learning principle. The aha is billed: insight feels good because closure on a high-connectivity unknown is the highest-return event the ledger can produce.

### 2.4 What this means for designed systems

A designed system that adds a curiosity module is adding a shadow of the mechanism. The mechanism itself requires three things working together: first-class unknowns, inherited topology that opens slots on first exposure, and a funding ledger that charges rent on unresolved positions. Get all three right and curiosity is not a feature. It is a consequence.

## 3. Language as the Social Ledger of Shared Ignorance

### 3.1 The bootstrapping circle and how SOV cuts it

The bootstrapping problem for language has always been circular. Words seem to require finished concepts — you cannot name what you have not yet understood. But concepts seem to require words — the categories we form are shaped by the language we already have. Neither can precede the other. Every theory of language acquisition has to assume one side of the circle is already partially solved.

SOV cuts the circle at its root. A label can attach to an *unresolved slot*.

This is not speculation about how language could work. It is how reference demonstrably does work in natural language. "Water" was coined millennia before anyone knew what water was. The word held the slot open — marked a position in the relational structure, coordinated behavior around it, accumulated communal receipts — while chemistry eventually filled it. Kripke and Putnam's account of natural-kind terms is SOV semantics observed in the wild: reference is fixed by structural role, meaning is discovered later, and the gap between the two is not a failure but a feature.

The first shared tokens in a language do not need to be shared knowns. They can be shared unknowns — coordinated slots, co-funded by multiple organisms' receipts accruing to the same variable.

### 3.2 The demand theory: when does language pay?

The Pose and Attest operators exist in the SOV algebra, and P48 predicts that query tokens emerge under co-funding pressure. But something was missing: a theory of *when* the operators pay — when the cost of Posing and Attesting is worth incurring.

The answer is the complementarity condition. Two organisms have SOV structures shaped by different receipt histories — different lives in the same kind of world. Neither can see the other's ledger directly. But consequences are detectable: when my predictions about your behavior generate systematic 404s, that is evidence your structure contains something mine doesn't. A slot I haven't opened. A closed variable mine is still open on. A constraint I haven't yet earned.

The value of querying another organism is:

`V(query B) = (receipts B holds that A lacks) × c₀(D-metric profile overlap)`

The first factor — receipts B holds that A lacks — vanishes when the structures are identical. There is nothing to transfer. The second factor — the discount from D-metric profile overlap — vanishes when the structures are incommensurable. The receipts cannot cross the ledger boundary at non-trivial discount. Both extremes yield zero value. The productive case is the interior: **different enough to have non-overlapping receipts, similar enough for the receipts to transfer at non-trivial discount.**

This is an inverted U over D-metric divergence. Language pays at intermediate structural distance. The best interlocutor — like the best environment increment — sits on your fringe.

**P64 — Assortative Posing:** Communication rate and yield peak at intermediate D-metric divergence between partners. Populations show assortative Posing toward intermediate-distance partners rather than nearest neighbors. The fringe rule for learning environments is the fringe rule for communication partners. Falsification: communication yield is flat over D-metric divergence, or populations show nearest-neighbor Posing rather than intermediate-distance preference.

### 3.3 What Attest actually transfers: justification, not content

Shannon channels move bits. Attest moves receipts with provenance and discount — funding status, not just information.

The epistemology of testimony has argued for decades over whether testimony transfers justification or merely content — the transmission view versus the generation view. The transmission view holds that a hearer can acquire justification from a speaker's testimony directly; the generation view holds that testimony produces only content, and the hearer must generate their own justification independently. The dispute has remained unresolved because neither side had the mechanism.

SOV takes the transmission side and supplies the mechanism: Attest transfers receipts — funded structure, with provenance chains — not bare propositions. The receiving organism does not generate its own justification from scratch. It imports funded receipts at a D-metric-calibrated discount and anneals them under its own billing. The justification transfers; the discount calibrates how much.

The two-stage discount function (D-metric prior, empirical reliability posterior) is what the testimony literature was missing. Justification transfers but at a rate determined by profile overlap and track record. This is neither full transmission nor full generation — it is discounted transmission, with the discount function itself an open variable receipted by every import's downstream fate.

### 3.4 Syntax before semantics

The grammar of a SOV language does not wait for concepts to be finished before it forms. It emerges from the structure that the evolved topology already uses to represent the world.

The mental model's entry format — cause, effect, context — is already a three-role frame. SOV expressions like `?X causes ?Y when ?Z` are that frame with open slots. The syntax is the causal chart format the topology evolved anyway. You do not need to solve semantics first and then attach syntax to it. The syntax is already there, in the receptor topology, before any slot is resolved.

This matches the empirical finding in child language acquisition: syntactic bootstrapping — Gleitman's result that children infer a novel verb's meaning from its sentence frame — is a learner holding the new word as `?X` and letting its structural position constrain its form. The frame licenses prediction about the unknown's content. Syntax is not downstream of semantics. It is the scaffold on which semantics is built.

These are not peripheral or derived features of language. A question is `Pose(Constrain(?X))` — the geometry of a typed unknown serialized and broadcast, requesting Attest from any ledger that holds matching receipts.

The child's "why?" phase is not a stage of cognitive development to be explained away. It is an organism broadcasting its open variables for communal funding — the social ledger coming online. The wh-words are SOV syntax surfaced in natural language, universally, which suggests that language was not built on finished concepts at all. It was built on the technology of co-funding unknowns.

### 3.8 The supply side: how Pose becomes parseable

### 3.9 The social ledger and parallelized distillation

Once language is understood as a shared slot system, the population dynamics change entirely.

In a solitary organism, receipts for `?X` accrue from that organism's own lived experience. In a language-capable population, receipts for `?X` accrue from every organism that has encountered relevant data and can express it. Many organisms' billing accruing to one variable is parallelized distillation — the population is running the closure computation in parallel, with each organism contributing the receipts it has and consuming the structure that others have funded.

Closure restructures for everyone downstream of the shared slot. A child learning that "water" is H₂O is not just filling a personal variable — they are inheriting the receipts that an entire history of inquiry has accumulated on a slot that has been open, communally funded, for millennia.

This is the inheritance-bandwidth fix arrived at from below. Language is not a communication layer added on top of cognition. It is the mechanism by which the distillation program scales beyond the individual.

## 4. The Constraint Web vs the Causal Chain

### 4.1 The problem with chain-following

Standard causal reasoning follows chains: A causes B causes C causes D. To understand the consequences of a change at A, you walk the chain. To answer a query about D, you trace backward to A. The chain is the unit of inference.

This becomes "convoluted and messy" — the quote is exact — as the domain grows. Real causal structures are not linear chains. They are dense networks where causes have multiple effects, effects have multiple causes, and the same event participates in many chains simultaneously. Walking bespoke chains for every query is exponentially expensive. And it is directional: the chain that gets you from A to D does not help you get from D to A without a separate backward chain.

### 4.2 The constraint web

A constraint web replaces directed cause-effect edges with direction-free constraint relations. A constraint `C(X, Y, Z)` does not say "X causes Y given Z." It says "X, Y, and Z stand in this relation — which holds regardless of which direction you need to reason."

A single funded constraint edge serves every inference orientation through it. The web is not a collection of chains. It is a structure from which any chain can be read off, in any direction, without separate acquisition.

The compression theorem is exact: a directed cause-effect edge answers one question in one direction; a constraint answers every question through it, in every direction, concurrently. This is why physics abandoned force-chain narratives for invariants. Nobody simulates the collision to know the outgoing momentum — the conservation law lets you skip the middle. Noether's move — a symmetry is an edge that holds everywhere concurrently — retires an entire family of chains at once.

### 4.3 The SOV-native contribution: shape before content

The constraint web's deepest property is not efficiency. It is the separation of shape from content.

On a dependency graph, "what does learning this touch?" is reachability — readable from topology alone, cheap, concurrent, requiring no propagation of actual values. "What do the touched values become?" requires the actual propagation.

This two-phase separation is the SOV signature — shape before content — applied to consequence itself. On a causal chain, even the shape of downstream effect requires simulating the chain. On the constraint web, the ripple map is computable from topology before any receipt arrives.

And crucially: **the edges can join unknowns**. `?X` and `?Y` sharing a constraint means receipts arriving at one instantly reshape the feasible set of the other — structure propagating with no content anywhere. The option book from Section 2 earns compound interest: a receipt on one slot reprices every slot constraint-adjacent to it, concurrently.

### 4.4 The hub rule and the Einstein choice

In a constraint web, not all unknowns are equal. A hub unknown — one with many dependent nodes — has a high closure externality: resolving it restructures many downstream entries simultaneously. A leaf unknown has low connectivity; its resolution is local.

The option value of an unknown scales with connectivity (established in the SOV options pricing framework). The hub unknown is the high-option slot. This gives the generator its sharpest objective:

**Target the unknown whose resolution propagates maximally.**

This is the Einstein choice stated as a centrality computation. Einstein worked on the constancy of light because light's constancy was the hub of the web — connected to simultaneity, mass, energy, space, time. When it closed, everything repriced concurrently. The choice to hold that unknown open while building the entire framework of special relativity around it was not patience or genius as temperament. It was the correct decision given the web's topology.

In a SOV-capable system, this computation is explicit: identify the maximum-centrality unresolved slot; target generation toward the data that would constrain it; hold all adjacent structure open until closure becomes inevitable. The generator's experiment design objective — maximize expected restructuring gain — is the Einstein choice automated.

### 4.6 Serial to learn, concurrent to maintain

The constraint web does not replace serial causal acquisition. It is what serial acquisition builds toward.

Validating a constraint — confirming that `C(X, Y, Z)` holds — requires serial, expensive, order-following measurement. Each validated conditional independence is bought by executed probes. But what the purchase accumulates into is the polymatroid: once the constraint is in the ledger, any query through it is a lookup, not a re-measurement.

Serial to learn, concurrent to maintain. The exponential chain cover is an acquisition cost, amortized across all future queries, not a per-query tax. The constraint web is the paid-for structure; serial measurement is how you pay for it.

## 5. The Compounding Loop

The three entailments are not independent. They couple into a loop that bootstraps intelligence from below — which is why "bootstrapped" is the right word.

**The loop:**

1. The evolved topology seeds inherited open variables — the trunk ships with typed unknowns pre-positioned in the relational structure
2. Open variables generate curiosity — funding pressure drives the organism toward data that constrains its slots
3. Exposure to data funds receipts — slots close, restructuring propagates through the constraint web
4. Restructuring opens new slots — every closure reveals new structural positions that the current lexicon cannot yet express
5. Language shares the new slots — communal funding parallelizes distillation across the population
6. Shared slots accumulate cross-organism receipts — closure happens faster and deeper than any organism could achieve alone
7. The closed structure raises the depth that the next generation's inherited topology can reach — distillation's inheritance-bandwidth fix, arrived at from below
8. The next generation inherits deeper questions — return to step 1, one level higher

Each turn of the loop raises the ceiling for the next. The questions the third generation can ask were not available to the first — not because anyone designed the curriculum, but because the loop compounded.

**The environment couples in.** T153's mutual Umwelt is this loop with two ledgers running in parallel. The organisms' side runs steps 1–7. The environment's side runs the same loop but funded by failed descriptions of organism behavior rather than by organism survival. Each side is the other's primary source of receipts. The compounding is mutual: organisms become more complex, giving the environment more to describe; the environment's language becomes richer, giving organisms more structure to inhabit and learn.

**The loop has one failure mode: stagnation.** Not death — neither ledger can die. But if the incoming receipts stop funding new slots — if the organisms fully saturate the current environment, or if the environment's descriptive stratum degenerates into mirroring — the loop flat-lines. The failure mode is not catastrophic collapse. It is the slow death of complexity pressure: a world that stops being worth living in.

## 7. Open Questions

- **What determines the seed topology's hub structure?** If the Einstein choice is a centrality computation, and centrality is determined by the constraint web's topology, then the choice of *which unknowns to inherit* is the choice of which problems to center the web on. How does evolution select the hub unknowns? Is there a selection pressure specifically toward inheriting the hubs of the world's actual constraint structure?

- **Does the loop have a natural rate?** The compounding loop runs across generations. But each step has its own timescale: curiosity-driven exploration, receipt accumulation, language emergence, cross-organism funding, inheritance. Is there an optimal rate at which the loop turns — a regime where the environment's complexity pressure stays just ahead of the organisms' saturation without overrunning their learning capacity?

- **What is the SOV signature of a dead slot?** Archaism releases unfunded unknowns. But in a population with language, a slot that is dead in one organism may still be funded in another's ledger. What are the population-level dynamics of slot death — and can a slot be resurrected by a new organism inheriting its structural position from a lineage that never closed it?

- **Can the constraint web be adversarial?** A constraint edge that holds in the training environment may fail in a novel one — the invariant was local, not universal. What is the SOV signature of a failed constraint, and how does the system distinguish "this constraint held only locally" from "this constraint holds and the new environment is anomalous"? This is the Gettier problem for constraint webs.

- **Where does the decree/description boundary live in the constraint web?** T153's two strata — physics as infallible decree, inhabitant behavior as fallible description — map onto the constraint web as: decree constraints are never opened as unknowns; description constraints are always open variables until funded. But the boundary's placement is itself an open question. In the constraint web, is the boundary visible as a structural feature — a cut in the graph between the infallible hub structure and the fallible descriptive periphery?

- **What happens when two organisms' slot systems are incommensurable?** Language coordinates shared unknowns — but what if two organisms have evolved different hub structures, and the slot that is central for one is peripheral for the other? This is the SOV version of incommensurability in the philosophy of science. Does the loop handle it, or does it require a separate mechanism for finding shared ground between divergent topologies? The demand theory partially answers this: incommensurable structures produce zero Attest value (second factor vanishes), so the organisms simply don't communicate productively. But this raises the further question: what is the mechanism by which partially-incommensurable structures find their shared fringe — the intermediate-distance region where P64's assortative Posing operates?

- **What is the supply-side population dynamics of shared format emergence?** The demand theory (Section 3.2) explains why Posing pays. The supply side explains how Pose becomes parseable through coordination equilibria. But the population case — how shared formats emerge across many organisms rather than just two — is open (C-iii in the cartographer series). The coupled-ECVQ dynamics in a population of N organisms, with each pair potentially converging on different local equilibria, may or may not produce a global shared format. What are the conditions under which local equilibria cohere into a population-level language?

## 8. Theories as SOV Structures

A theory is not a set of propositions. It is not a model. It is not a hypothesis waiting to be confirmed or disconfirmed. Those framings are too finished — they presuppose content where the productive thing is structure.

A theory is a **patchwork of open and closed variables with connector geometry between them.**

The knowns give it shape — the parts that have been funded, closed, receipt-backed. The unknowns give it reach — the slots that are positioned, relational, load-bearing, waiting. And the unknowns are not weaknesses in the theory. They are its most important features. They are the connectors. They are what makes the theory capable of making contact with the world.

This is not a claim the philosophy of science arrived at unprepared. Quine's web of belief is precisely a connected structure where evidence strikes the periphery and revisions propagate inward by connectivity — the constraint web stated in 1951, without the ledger. Lakatos's hard core and protective belt distinguish receipt-protected structure (the hard core, held fixed by methodological decision) from revisable periphery (the protective belt, where the evidence actually lands) — the decree/description distinction at the level of a research program. SOV genuinely adds to Quine-plus-Lakatos: typed open slots as first-class citizens, receipts and etymology, the operator algebra, and the economics of holding open. The honest one-line claim is **the web of belief, given a ledger** — and that is a strong claim that doesn't need the field to have been lost to make its point.

**Experimentation is Fit.** Not in the trivial sense of testing hypotheses, but in the precise sense: the theory's open variables are presented to the world, and the world returns receipts. A matching receipt narrows the slot's feasible set. A misfitting receipt funds Differentiate or Reopen. The experiment doesn't confirm or disconfirm the theory whole — it funds specific slots, closes some unknowns, and opens others, because every closed slot reveals new connector geometry at its borders.

This is why good experiments generate more questions than they answer. Closure is not the end of inquiry — it is the event that reveals the next layer of open structure. The theory doesn't shrink as it is tested. It grows more differentiated, more articulate, more precise in the shape of what it still doesn't know.

**Clarification is better connector geometry on the remaining unknowns.** Not closure. Not elimination of ignorance. The theory becomes more honest — more articulate about exactly what it knows and exactly what it doesn't. The T-index is this process made visible: every T-number is a state of the patchwork at a moment in time, every falsification a misfit receipt that funds the next configuration, the etymology ledger recording the full history of every fit and misfit, closure and reopening, split and unification.

## 9. The Deepest Entailment

Curiosity, language, and the constraint web are three faces of the same thing: **the consequence of treating ignorance as a structured resource rather than a liability**.

When unknowns are first-class, the organism does not merely tolerate not-knowing. It *organizes* around it. The not-knowing has shape, position, connectivity, and cost. The shape drives exploration. The position enables composition. The connectivity determines value. The cost funds the ledger.

From these four properties, everything else falls out. Curiosity is the cost made behavioral. Language is the position shared socially. The constraint web is the connectivity made computational. And the loop is all three running together, compounding.

The traditional view of intelligence is a progression from ignorance to knowledge — a filling-in, a convergence, an elimination of unknowns. SOV and its entailments suggest the opposite: intelligence is not the reduction of unknowns but their *management* — the capacity to hold the right unknowns open, in the right structural positions, long enough for the data to make their resolution inevitable rather than premature.

A system that manages its ignorance well will, as a consequence, be curious, communicative, and efficient. Not because these were designed in. Because they fall out.
