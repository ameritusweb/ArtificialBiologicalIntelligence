# The Implication Organ — Requirements
## Theory-driven genesis: making closures fertile (T163; P120–P121)

*Design doc, 2026-08-14. Build follows this doc, phased; no phase begins
until its predecessor bills. The organ is the program's own methodology —
experiment → findings → implications → next experiments — as anatomy; its
reflexive receipt is the findings-implications ritual that built the last
thirty findings by hand.*

---

## 1. Demand (C10)

Every genesis channel is world-driven: Individuate opens slots from the
404 pool, Compose from co-fit windows, Differentiate from near-miss
strain. The organism never opens a slot because two of its own Ks jointly
imply something untested. The consequences are measured, not
hypothetical: closures accumulate as sterile stock (the rut with a full
ledger), and Φ — onward fertility, T160 vii's third worth axis — is never
*realized*, only possessed. T158 gave the organism a way to spend its
failures; nothing spends its successes. The implication organ is the
settlement-side twin of the why-economy, and the second genesis class:
DEDUCTIVE (theory-driven) beside the standing INDUCTIVE (world-driven).

## 2. The organ in the design grammar (C5–C9)

- **Currency (C6):** joint implications — claims licensed by a
  conjunction of Ks that neither member licenses alone, including joint
  CONFLICTS (two Ks contradicting is an implication: an experiment to
  resolve). The loop closes: a tested implication becomes a closure,
  which triggers the next pass.
- **Seed receptors (C5, C8):**
  1. `new_closure` — fires on a billed closure event (the ritual's
     trigger; veering endpoints feed the WHY-side, not this one).
  2. `conjunction_tension` — fires when a retrieved pair jointly
     predicts or jointly conflicts (the felt form of "these two things
     together mean something").
- **Effectors:** minting LIENED open slots carrying pre-registered
  content — the organism's own P-registry. The lien operator (standing)
  marks them provisional until the designed experiment bills.
- **Mental model:** a map of conjunction SHAPES to realized fertility —
  which kinds of pairings (strata, distances, class volumes) historically
  produced implications that funded. The organism learning to ask better
  generative questions is this map maturing; it is the loop's
  self-improvement rung.
- **Couplings (C9):** the organ's minted slots enter the experiment
  organ's nomination stream through a weight initialized at zero —
  selection bills whether listening to theory pays.

## 3. The loop

1. **TRIGGER**: `new_closure` fires (boundary-batched; see cadence).
2. **RETRIEVE**: standing Ks at INTERMEDIATE distance from the new
   closure (T162's annulus at K grain; metric in §4), capped at C
   candidates (annulus + cap defeats the O(K²) conjunction explosion).
3. **CONJOIN** in the imagination register: derive the joint prediction
   or surface the joint conflict. Register-only — no ledger contact.
4. **PRICE** by W(D): expected discharge × onward fertility,
   class-volume weighted (F63: wide classes are cheap and worth less).
5. **MINT**: open a liened slot with the pre-registered content;
   provenance records BOTH parents' receipt sets (the conjunction's
   genealogy — P121's data, kept by construction).
6. **EXIT** to the experiment organ as a designed intervention (the
   register's exit rule enacted: structures never exit, questions do).
   The eventual bill funds or kills the lien; either way the outcome
   updates the conjunction-shape map (stage 4's prior).

**Cadence**: the boundary reflection phase — the low-demand replay
period. Holding-without-acting is a PHASE, not only a place: findings
are compared where the organism already replays (sleep does the
conjoining).

## 4. The distance over knowledge (the load-bearing choice)

A K's behavioral identity is its PERTURBATION-RESPONSE SPECTRUM — which
doses reopen it, which strata it survives. This is T161's law at
knowledge grain: the fragility spectroscopy (F25/F28) was the collapse
fingerprint of Ks before either had the name.

- **Primary metric — CO-RESPONSE DISTANCE**: similarity of two Ks'
  responses across the HISTORICAL perturbation record. Every lineage run
  already stamped every K with reopened-or-survived, strained-or-quiet,
  per world event: a K × event response matrix, free on the ledger.
  Stratum-tunable — the fragility strata are the material dial at K
  grain (near in furniture-response, far in structure-response is a
  meaningful, probe-chosen fact).
- **Hard inner-edge guard — PROVENANCE OVERLAP**: Ks funded by shared
  evidence make CIRCULAR conjunctions (double-counted support, F48's
  effective-n lesson at claim grain). Zero provenance distance is
  RUMINATION, formally — whys discharging each other in a
  shared-evidence cycle — so the retrieval metric is Law 3's firewall,
  applied before fertility is even considered.
- **Outer edge**: uncorrelated spectra share NO LEVER — no single
  intervention the experiment organ can design moves both, so the joint
  prediction is unbillable (conservativity's geometry). Not wrong;
  untestable. Excluded by the annulus, not by fiat.
- **Cold start (C8 pattern)**: young Ks have no perturbation history —
  geometry (threshold profiles) bootstraps, provenance guards from
  birth, co-response dominates as the record accumulates.

## 5. Safety (inherited whole, none invented)

The register's exit rule (an implication becomes an experiment, never a
belief); liens until billed (C2 by construction — implication-born
structure cannot fund anything, including other implications, until the
world pays it); the annulus inner edge as anti-rumination; minting
priced against REALIZED funding (quest-inflation guard at claim grain —
an organ whose implications never bill loses its minting budget); the
young-ledger law for the organ's voice in the shared stream.

## 6. Phases (each bills before the next)

- **IO-0 — the retrospective instrument** (read-only; no organism
  change): (a) compute the K × event co-response matrix from an existing
  lineage web's ledger; deliverable = the distance metric commissioned,
  distributions inspected, provenance-overlap guard validated against
  known-circular pairs. (b) P121's retro: score this program's OWN
  findings ledger — were the fertile conjunctions (implications that
  later billed) at intermediate co-response distance, low provenance
  overlap? The organ's design validated on its own discoverer's record.
- **IO-1 — passive minting**: run the loop through stage 5 only (mint
  liened slots, no experiment handoff); endpoint = do implication-born
  slots, left to ambient contact, fund at ≥ the rate of matched
  world-born slots? (The weak form of P120 — theory finds fundable
  holes even without designed tests.)
- **IO-2 — the full loop**: exit to the experiment organ; designed
  tests for minted implications (requires the experiment organ's
  body-currency venue — the physics world; the two organs commission
  together there).
- **IO-3 — P120's bill**: matched-budget race, deductive+inductive
  genesis vs inductive alone; endpoints = implication-slot funding rate
  and venue R0.

## 7. Pre-registered risks and kills

- **Conjunction explosion**: O(K²) pairs — the annulus + cap + boundary
  batching bound it; if the candidate stream still floods, the
  info-priced threshold lesson (F49) applies before any build widens.
- **Lien inflation**: minting without funding is quest inflation at
  claim grain — the pricing loop (stage 4 fed by stage 6 outcomes) is
  the guard, and IO-1's endpoint measures it before IO-2 spends
  experiments.
- **Trivial implications**: conjunctions can be logically valid and
  empirically empty; the W(D) discharge term (would the bill retire any
  hazard?) is the filter, and the conjunction-shape map should learn to
  starve empty forms.
- **C15**: all phases run the full organism; the reflection phase is
  added cadence, not a reduced venue.

## 8. What this organ completes

With it, the loop the program has run BY HAND closes inside the
organism: live (worlds) → learn (closures) → ask (conjunctions) → mint
(liened predictions) → test (experiment organ) → close → ask again. The
organism stops being a learner that happens to accumulate knowledge and
becomes a researcher that spends it. The last line of the methodology it
implements is the user's: findings → implications → next experiments —
and the first thing it should ever be pointed at is its own ledger.
